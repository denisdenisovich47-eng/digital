01 / The role

What does a digital
marketer do?

A digital marketer promotes products and services on the Internet, connects brands with people, and measures how well campaigns work.

Creative work meets data
↗

Promote online

Help products and services reach the right audience.

✳

Create campaigns

Make engaging posts, videos and online advertisements.

▥

Analyse results

Check views, engagement and what affects decisions.

◎

Grow a business

Attract potential customers and support sales.

02 / Why this career?

Creative thinking.
Real results.

I have chosen this career because I am interested in technology, business and social media. I enjoy learning how online platforms work and why some advertisements become popular while others do not.

I think this profession suits me because I am curious, creative and ready to learn. I like solving problems and finding different ways to reach a goal.

03 / My career goals

One step at a time.

My plan is to build a strong foundation first, then gain practical experience and gradually become a professional.

Short-term

Finish school

Get good marks in my exams, improve my English and learn more about computers and advertising.

Mid-term

Study marketing

Enter a university or college, take online courses and practise by creating real projects.

Long-term

Become a specialist

Work on interesting campaigns, develop my skills and maybe start my own digital marketing agency.

05 / A balanced view

The good & the
challenging.

Like every profession, digital marketing has advantages and disadvantages. Knowing both sides helps me prepare.

↗

Advantages

  • Creative work and new ideas
  • Flexible hours and possible remote work
  • Career development and promotion
  • Constant opportunities to learn
!

Challenges

  • Pressure and strict deadlines
  • Trends and tools change quickly
  • Campaigns may not meet expectations
  • You need to keep learning throughout your career
06 / My dream job
